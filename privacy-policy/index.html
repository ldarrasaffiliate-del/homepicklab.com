Privacy policy

This policy explains what data we collect, how cookies and consent work, and how you can control analytics and external media on HomePicksLab.com.

What we collect

  • Emails you send us: if you contact us, we receive your email address and message.
  • Basic server logs: standard technical logs may be generated by the hosting provider.
  • Analytics (optional): only if you accept Analytics in the cookie banner.

Cookies & consent choices

We use a consent banner with three categories:

  • Necessary (always on): core functionality and consent storage.
  • Analytics (GA4) (optional): helps us understand aggregated site usage.
  • External media (optional): enables YouTube embeds (youtube-nocookie.com).

You can change your choices any time via “Manage cookies” in the footer.

Analytics (Google Analytics 4)

GA4 is only loaded after you give consent for Analytics. Measurement ID: G-W6JW3C08M3.

If you reject Analytics, GA4 is not loaded and no GA4 requests are made by the site.

External media (YouTube)

Videos are embedded using youtube-nocookie.com. If you do not allow External media, video iframes are blocked and replaced by a “Click to load video” placeholder.

You can also allow External media just for your current session by clicking “Load video” on a blocked embed.

Affiliate links

Some links are affiliate links and may be marked as sponsored (rel="sponsored nofollow"). See the disclosure on the Legal notice page.

Your rights & contact

Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, or delete your personal data. To make a request, email l.darras.affiliate@gmail.com.
