Choose in 5 minutes

  1. Counter space: Foodi MAX is wider (side-by-side). DoubleStack is slimmer in width but taller.
  2. What you cook: lots of sides/snacks vs bigger items. Basket shape matters as much as total liters/quarts.
  3. Two foods, one finish: both lines often offer Sync/Match-style modes—verify for your exact model.
  4. Accessories: racks, extra crisper plates, or a thermometer can change the value—confirm what’s included.
  5. Cleaning: two drawers = two routines. Pick the one you’ll actually maintain.

Pick Foodi MAX if…

  • You want easy access to both drawers (side-by-side).
  • You cook for a family and batch cook often.
  • You have enough counter width for a wider appliance.

Pick DoubleStack XL if…

  • Your counter is narrow and footprint matters more than height.
  • You like the idea of a vertical setup with two drawers.
  • You want a compact-looking station for two-zone cooking.

FAQ

Is the vertical DoubleStack “better”?

It’s better if your main constraint is counter width. If you have room, the side-by-side Foodi MAX may feel simpler day to day because both baskets are at the same height.

Are they big enough for a family?

Often yes, but “family size” depends on your portions and what you cook. Confirm the basket volume and usable cooking area on the listing.

What’s the #1 thing to check?

Basket shape + accessories. Two models can share the same total liters/quarts and still cook very differently depending on depth, width, and included racks.

Do I need a smart thermometer?

Only if you’ll use it. It’s great for meats, but many people never plug it in. If precision matters to you, choose a bundle that includes it and confirm compatibility.
